# The Complete Yachtmaster — contents reference

Tom Cunliffe’s *The Complete Yachtmaster*, **10th edition (2021)**.

This reference records the chapter listings and sources found on 25 October 2026. Page numbers below are the book’s **printed page numbers**, not PDF page numbers. They may differ from those in other editions.

## Sources and detailed subsection listings

- [Public book preview — 10th edition](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781472982971_A42450030/preview-9781472982971_A42450030.pdf): PDF pages **6–8** contain the detailed contents, including subsection headings and their printed page numbers, for all 32 chapters and both appendices. These contents pages were checked directly.
- [Searchable webpage with chapter and subsection listings](https://www.morkniga.ru/p841016.html): lists the detailed contents for the 2021 edition, but largely omits page numbers.
- [Cunliffe’s own book page — 11th edition](https://www.tomcunliffe.com/product/complete-yachtmaster-8th-ed/): confirms the same 32 chapter titles. Despite the older edition number in its URL, the page identifies the book as the 11th edition.

The preview provides a verified subsection listing; this document transcribes the chapter list and one subsection example rather than the entire detailed contents.

## Use in the learning project

The detailed contents can help select manageable reading sections and organise questions by chapter and subsection. Differences between successive start pages give approximate reading lengths; sections can share a page, so these are not exact text lengths.

The contents establish the book’s structure, not its full treatment of each subject. Questions requiring exact correspondence with Cunliffe’s explanations should still be checked against the relevant text. Chapter coverage and coverage of the selected official RYA syllabus remain separate considerations.

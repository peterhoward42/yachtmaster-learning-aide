## Lighthouses

### 1. Identifying a light

At night, you expect to see a lighthouse charted as **Fl(3) W 15s**. Which observation matches?

A. A white light lasting three seconds, repeated every fifteen seconds.

B. Three white flashes forming a complete cycle every fifteen seconds.

C. A white flash every fifteen seconds, followed by three seconds of darkness.

D. Three white flashes, each fifteen seconds apart.

**My answer (A–D):** D

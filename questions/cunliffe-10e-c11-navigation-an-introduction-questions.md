# Navigation – an introduction — provisional starter questions

Six topic-based questions provisionally associated with chapter 11 of Tom Cunliffe’s *The Complete Yachtmaster*, **10th edition (2021), printed pages 103–106**.

## Scope status

The repository records the chapter title and page span but no subsection headings or chapter text. These questions therefore sample introductory navigation concepts selected for this draft; they are **not verified against the chapter’s actual scope or explanations**. They should be revised after checking the book, rather than treated as evidence of chapter coverage.

The [coverage model](../cunliffe-coverage-model.md) proposes six questions: four associated with the whole chapter, C11.S01, and two integration questions. This draft follows that count. Detailed chart work, tidal calculations, compass corrections, position fixing and passage planning have their own later chapters and are left for those sets.

Provisional objectives:

- Distinguish knowing a position from establishing a safe route.
- Distinguish heading from movement over the ground.
- Relate speed over the ground, distance and elapsed time.
- Understand why a position’s age and uncertainty matter.
- Connect motion over the ground to a boat-handling decision.
- Use a prediction to expose a disagreement between navigational information and observations.

## Instructions

- Questions 1, 2 and 4 have three options and one best answer. Questions 3, 5 and 6 need a short written answer.
- Distances are nautical miles; a knot is one nautical mile per hour. All directions use the same north reference.
- The answer key is omitted for the initial attempt. No confidence ratings are needed.
